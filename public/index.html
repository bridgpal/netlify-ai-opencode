Netlify AI relay

This site forwards requests to Netlify's AI Gateway for tools such as OpenCode. Every endpoint requires a relay key, so there is nothing to see here without one.

Endpoints: /anthropic/*, /openai/*, /gemini/*, /openrouter/*, /models, /whoami.

curl -H "Authorization: Bearer $RELAY_KEY" https://<this-site>/whoami

Source and setup: github.com/bridgpal/netlify-ai-opencode
